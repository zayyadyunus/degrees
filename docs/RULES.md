# What counts as a connection

In **Degrees** you build a chain from the start word to the target using the
tiles on the board, and each tile must connect to the word before it by a
*real semantic relationship*. A move lands when the two words relate in one of
the ways below. Rhyming, spelling, and "it just reminds me of…" don't count.

## The ways to connect

Think of each move as filling in one of these blanks:

| # | Relationship | Reads as… | Examples |
|---|--------------|-----------|----------|
| 1 | **Same meaning** | "is another word for" | sea ↔ ocean · film ↔ movie · rock ↔ stone |
| 2 | **Opposite** | "is the opposite of" | hot ↔ cold · love ↔ hate · war ↔ peace |
| 3 | **Is a kind of** | "is a type of" | dog → animal · violin → instrument · apple → fruit |
| 4 | **Same category** | "is the same kind of thing as" | fork ↔ spoon · lion ↔ tiger · gold ↔ silver |
| 5 | **Part of** | "is a part of" | wheel → car · leaf → tree · finger → hand |
| 6 | **Made of** | "is made of" | wine → grape · glass → sand · honey → bee |
| 7 | **Used for / does** | "is used to ___" | hammer → nail · lamp → light · ship → sea |
| 8 | **Found together** | "you find one near the other" | bee → flower · sand → beach · star → sky |
| 9 | **Leads to / comes from** | "causes" or "comes from" | fire → smoke · seed → tree · egg → chicken |

A tenth, looser kind also counts when it's well known:

| 10 | **Stands for** | "is a symbol of" | crown → king · heart → love · white → peace |

Strong everyday associations that don't fit a pattern neatly can count too —
the game marks those links **related**. Every puzzle can be solved at par
using only the ten patterns above, so you never *need* a loose link.

## What does NOT count

- **Rhymes / sounds alike** — *cat / hat*, *moon / spoon*
- **Spelled alike** — *sale / sail*, *cat / cot*
- **Starts with the same letter** — not a meaning at all
- **Personal associations** — "my street is named Oak, so street / oak"
- **Weak hunches** — "it just reminds me of…" isn't enough; the two words
  have to be closely associated for most people

## Tips

- When you're stuck, run down the list: *Is there a synonym? An opposite? A
  category? A part? Something it's made of, used for, found with, or leads to?*
- Work from both ends: several tiles may link to the target, but not all of
  them can be reached from the start in par.
- **Par** is the fewest moves the puzzle can be solved in. Match it for a ⛳.
- Not every tile helps. Some are dead ends — **Back** undoes your last step,
  but the move still counts towards your score and your 8-move budget.
- A tile that doesn't connect costs a **strike**; three strikes and the
  puzzle is lost.
- **Hint** reveals the next tile on a shortest route from where you are. It's
  always available, but hints show on your result.
- Win or lose, the end screen reveals a par route.
